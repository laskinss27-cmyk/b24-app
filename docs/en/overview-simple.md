# What this system is

[Русская версия](../overview-simple.md)

Employees continue working in Bitrix24, but use our purpose-built screens instead of inconvenient standard interfaces:

- deal products and documents;
- product catalogue;
- procurement;
- warehouse operations;
- stocktaking;
- repairs;
- marketplaces;
- reports and contracts.

Two systems operate underneath the interface:

- Bitrix24 knows about customers, deals, tasks, and files;
- ERPNext knows about products, warehouses, stock levels, and inventory documents.

Our application connects them and presents the result in one place.

The system runs continuously on the corporate server. A user's computer is only needed to open Bitrix24; the server, inventory core, backups, and other employees' access do not depend on it.

If our application is temporarily unavailable, standard Bitrix24 remains a separate system. If the inventory core is unavailable, warehouse operations must not be recorded from memory: wait for recovery and check whether the document may already have been created.
